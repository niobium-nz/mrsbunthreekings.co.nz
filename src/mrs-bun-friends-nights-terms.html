Offer name: Mrs Bun Friends Nights
Promoter: Mrs Bun Three Kings
Store: Mrs Bun Three Kings, 540D Mount Albert Road, Three Kings, Auckland 1042

1. Offer summary

Eligible returning customers may receive 50% off one selected dish of the day at Mrs Bun Three Kings on qualifying promotional nights.

The selected dishes are:

Day Eligible item
Monday Beef Noodle Soup
Wednesday Buns, any available menu size
Friday Kung Po Chicken
Sunday Dumplings, any available menu size

The offer applies only to the eligible item listed for that specific day. Items cannot be swapped across days.

2. Campaign period

The offer is valid every Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday after 6pm during the promotional period stated by Mrs Bun Three Kings.

Campaign period:

Wednesday 1 July 2026 to Monday 31 August 2026

Mrs Bun Three Kings may extend, amend, suspend, or end the promotion at any time, subject to applicable law.

3. Participating store

This offer is valid at Mrs Bun Three Kings only.

Address: 540D Mount Albert Road, Three Kings, Auckland 1042

The offer is not available at any other location unless expressly stated by Mrs Bun Three Kings.

4. Eligible products

The offer applies only to the selected dish of the day:

Monday: Beef Noodle Soup
Wednesday: Buns, any available menu size
Friday: Kung Po Chicken
Sunday: Dumplings, any available menu size

For buns and dumplings, “any size” means any standard menu size normally offered by Mrs Bun Three Kings on that day, subject to availability.

The offer does not apply to combo meals, drinks, sides, extra toppings, delivery fees, catering orders, third-party delivery orders, or any other products unless expressly stated.

5. Offer price

Eligible customers receive 50% off the regular menu price of one eligible selected dish of the day.

The regular menu price is the price normally charged by Mrs Bun Three Kings for that item at other times.

If a fixed promotional price is advertised in-store or online, the fixed promotional price will apply instead.

The discount applies to the eligible dish only. It does not apply to add-ons, sides, drinks, combo upgrades, service fees, delivery charges, public holiday surcharges, card surcharges, or other charges.

6. Returning customer requirement

This offer is available to returning Mrs Bun Three Kings customers only.

To redeem the offer, customers must show proof of a previous Mrs Bun Three Kings purchase made within the 1 month immediately before the redemption date.

Acceptable proof includes:

  • a previous Mrs Bun receipt;
  • an online order confirmation or screenshot showing a previous Mrs Bun Three Kings purchase;
  • a bank transaction showing a previous Mrs Bun Three Kings purchase.

The previous purchase must have been made before the day of redemption.

7. Privacy and proof of purchase

Staff only need to sight the proof of previous purchase.

Mrs Bun staff must not photograph, copy, record, or store customer proof of purchase.

Customers may cover or hide personal details, account numbers, balances, unrelated transactions, or other private information before showing proof.

Proof only needs to show enough information for staff to reasonably confirm that the customer has previously purchased from Mrs Bun Three Kings within the last 1 month.

8. Redemption limit

The offer is limited to one discounted eligible item per person per proof of purchase per qualifying promotional night.

A customer may redeem the offer once on Monday, once on Wednesday, once on Friday, and once on Sunday, provided they meet the proof-of-purchase requirement each time.

Mrs Bun Three Kings may refuse multiple claims from the same person on the same promotional night or using of redeemed proof of purchase.

Mrs Bun Three Kings may refuse claim from using the proof of purchase that refers to the same transaction and it has been previously redeemed.

9. Availability

The offer is available while stocks last.

Product availability may vary. Mrs Bun Three Kings may substitute, limit, or withdraw items if ingredients or products are unavailable.

No rain checks are available unless expressly stated by Mrs Bun Three Kings.

This offer cannot be used with:

  • other discounts;

  • delivery platform promotions;

The offer is available for in-store purchase only and dine-in only at Mrs Bun Three Kings.

It is not available through Uber Eats, DoorDash, Menulog, other delivery platforms, or any third-party ordering service unless expressly stated.

Mrs Bun Three Kings staff may refuse redemption if:

  • proof of a previous purchase is not shown;
  • the proof does not appear to relate to Mrs Bun Three Kings;

  • the customer attempts to redeem more than once on the same promotional night;
  • the customer attempts to redeem more than once on the same proof of purchase;
  • the customer attempts to redeem the wrong item for that day;
  • the offer is being misused;
  • the product is unavailable.

Staff should apply these conditions respectfully and consistently.
