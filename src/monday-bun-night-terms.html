Monday Bun Night — Terms & Conditions

Offer name: Monday Bun Night
Promoter: Mrs Bun Three Kings
Offer: Eligible returning customers may purchase one regular-size buns or dumplings item for $5.90 on Monday nights during the campaign period.

1. Campaign period

The offer is valid every Monday night during the promotional period stated by Mrs Bun Three Kings.

Unless extended or withdrawn earlier, the recommended trial period is:

Monday 11 May 2026 to Monday 29 June 2026.

Mrs Bun Three Kings may extend, amend, suspend, or end the promotion at any time, subject to applicable law.

2. Participating store

This offer is valid at:

Mrs Bun Three Kings only.

Address: 540D Mount Albert Road, Three Kings, Auckland 1042

The offer is not available at any other location unless expressly stated.

3. Eligible products

The offer applies only to the following regular-size items:

6-piece regular buns or 8-piece regular dumplings.

The offer does not apply to large sizes, combo meals, drinks, sides, delivery platform orders, catering orders, or any other products unless expressly stated.

4. Offer price

Eligible customers may purchase one eligible regular-size item for:

$5.90

The regular price is:

$11.80 at other times.

To avoid misleading pricing, Mrs Bun should only use “50% off” while $11.80 remains the genuine regular price charged at other times. The Commerce Commission warns that if a promotional price is used routinely, that promotional price may become the usual selling price. (Commerce Commission)

5. Returning customer requirement

This offer is available to returning Mrs Bun customers only.

To redeem the offer, customers must show proof of a previous Mrs Bun purchase from a prior day. Acceptable proof includes:

  • a previous Mrs Bun receipt;
  • an online order confirmation or screenshot;
  • a bank transaction showing a previous Mrs Bun purchase.

The previous purchase must have been made before the day of redemption.

6. Privacy and proof of purchase

Staff only need to sight the proof of previous purchase.

Mrs Bun staff must not photograph, copy, record, or store customer proof of purchase.

Customers may cover or hide personal details, account numbers, balances, unrelated transactions, or other private information before showing proof.

This approach is recommended because New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020 regulates how businesses collect, store, use, and share personal information. (Privacy Commissioner of New Zealand)

7. Redemption limit

The offer is limited to:

One discounted regular item per person per Monday night.

Mrs Bun may refuse multiple claims from the same person on the same Monday night.

8. Availability

The offer is available while stocks last.

Product availability may vary during the night. Mrs Bun Three Kings may substitute or withdraw items if ingredients or products are unavailable.

9. Not valid with other offers

This offer cannot be used with:

  • other discounts;
  • combo deals;
  • loyalty rewards;
  • vouchers;
  • delivery platform promotions;
  • third-party offers;
  • large-size upgrades.

10. In-store only

The offer is available for in-store purchase only at Mrs Bun Three Kings.

It is not available through Uber Eats, DoorDash, Menulog, other delivery platforms, or any third-party ordering service unless expressly stated.

11. Staff discretion

Mrs Bun staff may refuse redemption if:

  • proof of a previous purchase is not shown;
  • the proof does not appear to relate to Mrs Bun;
  • the customer attempts to redeem more than once on the same Monday night;
  • the offer is being misused;
  • the product is unavailable.

Staff should apply this condition respectfully and consistently.

12. No cash alternative

The offer has no cash value and cannot be exchanged for cash, credit, or another product.

13. Changes to the promotion

Mrs Bun Three Kings may change, suspend, or cancel the promotion if necessary, including due to stock shortages, operational issues, pricing errors, or circumstances beyond its control.

Any changes should be communicated clearly in-store or online.
